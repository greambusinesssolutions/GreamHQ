## Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted services to run this site. They process information on our behalf and only as we instruct:

- **Formspree** receives the answers you send through our forms and passes them to us by email.
- **Cal.com** handles booking when you choose a time for your call. It receives the name, email and booking details you give it.
- **Vercel** hosts this website. Its privacy-friendly analytics count page visits without cookies and without identifying you.
- **WhatsApp** (Meta) carries any message you choose to send us there.

Some of these services may store information outside your country. Where they do, we rely on the safeguards they provide, such as standard contractual clauses. We may also share information if the law requires it.
