Enquiries now arrive on WhatsApp, Instagram, email, your website and the phone. Each channel has its own inbox, and none of them talk to each other.

The fix is simple to describe: one list where every enquiry lands, one owner for each, and a follow-up date that someone is reminded about. Start with the channel that brings the most enquiries, then add the rest.
