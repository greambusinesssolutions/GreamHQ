Connecting a 60-room hotel from booking to payment taught us to start at the end: the bill. If every room charge and restaurant bill follows the guest to one account, the front desk can see the balance before check-out.

From there we worked backwards to bookings and payments, one step at a time, with the team using each part before we added the next.
