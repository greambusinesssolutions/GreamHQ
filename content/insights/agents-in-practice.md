This walkthrough looks at what AI agents actually do day to day in a growing business, and what a person still does. It’s written as the notes for a 30-minute session.

## Part 1: The enquiry that arrives at night

A customer messages at 9:42 PM asking about delivery and a bulk order. The agent replies straight away with the delivery days, notes that bulk prices need confirming, and drafts a price reply for the team to approve in the morning. The enquiry is logged and a follow-up is set.

**What a person does:** checks and approves the price before it’s sent.

## Part 2: The customer who went quiet

A customer asked for a quote and hasn’t replied. Two days later, the agent sends a short, friendly follow-up using a message the team wrote and approved in advance. If the customer replies with a question it can’t answer, it hands over to a person.

**What a person does:** writes and approves the follow-up messages once; handles anything unusual.

## Part 3: The payment that needs matching

Transfers arrive with inconsistent references. The agent matches each one to the right order where it’s certain, marks the order paid and sends a receipt. Anything that doesn’t match cleanly is flagged rather than guessed.

**What a person does:** reviews every flagged payment.

## Part 4: Measuring whether it’s worth it

Before switching an agent on, note how the job is done today: how long replies take, how many follow-ups are missed, how long matching payments takes. After a few weeks, compare. Keep what works; change or stop what doesn’t.

## The pattern

One job at a time. Clear limits. A person approving what matters. Results measured honestly.
