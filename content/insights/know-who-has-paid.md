If you check payments by scrolling through bank alerts and customers' screenshots, you're not alone. It's slow, and it's easy to miss a payment or chase one that's already been made.

The answer is a single list of orders with their payment status, updated as money arrives. Part payments and overdue balances should stand out without anyone looking for them.
