Most growing businesses don't run on software. They run on WhatsApp chats, a few spreadsheets, a payment app and the memory of one or two people.

That works while the business is small. As it grows, the gaps show: enquiries arrive in several places and some never get a reply, follow-ups depend on someone remembering, and checking who has paid means scrolling through screenshots.

Closing those gaps doesn't start with new software. It starts with seeing where customers and money slip, then putting enquiries, orders and payments in one place.
