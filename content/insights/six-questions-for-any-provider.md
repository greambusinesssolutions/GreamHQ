Before you sign with any software or AI provider, ask six questions. Which jobs will it do, exactly? What does a person still approve? Where does our data go, and who owns it? How will we measure if it works? What happens when it gets something wrong? Who keeps it working after launch?

Good providers answer all six clearly, in writing.
