The short answer

What is an AI agent, for a business like yours?

An AI agent takes one routine job off your team, like answering a common question or checking that a payment arrived. It works in the channels you already use, and hands anything unusual to a person. Start with the job that's frequent, rule-based and costly when it's missed.
